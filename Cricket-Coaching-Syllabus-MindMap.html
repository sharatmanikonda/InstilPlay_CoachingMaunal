      • Healthy relationship with failure
      • Concentration & the pre-ball routine

    • Ch 16 · Tactics & Captaincy
      • Reading the game (situation, conditions, batter, bowler)

      • Batting tactics (build & pace an innings)

      • Formats & junior modified games
      • Captaincy as a coachable skill

    • Ch 18 · Season & Group Planning
      • Annual periodisation (off / pre / in-season / transition)
      • Planning a block / term
      • Balancing the squad (all-round development)
      • Match management within the plan

  • PART 1 · Foundations of Coaching
    • Ch 1 · Coach’s Role & Philosophy
      • Four channels: environment, relationship, role model, instructor
      • Building a coaching philosophy (why / what / how)
      • Winning vs developing
      • Code of conduct & Spirit of Cricket
      • The reflective coach
    • Ch 2 · How Young Players Learn
      • LTAD stages (FUNdamentals → Active for Life)
      • Skill-hungry years (~8–12)
      • Growth, maturation & the awkward phase
      • Relative age effect & late developers
      • Attention spans & session pacing
    • Ch 3 · Coaching Methods
      • Game-based coaching (TGfU)
      • Constraints-led approach (task / environment / individual)
      • Blocked vs varied practice
      • Discovery vs instruction balance
    • Ch 4 · Communication & Feedback
      • External vs internal cues
      • Feedback: less, later, earned
      • Questioning
      • Praise the process (growth mindset)
      • Managing behaviour
    • Ch 5 · Parents, Team & Group
      • Working with parents / the car-ride home
      • Mixed abilities in one group
      • Inclusion as default
  • PART 2 · Safety & Care
    • Ch 6 · Safeguarding & Inclusion
      • Pre-season essentials (clearance, welfare officer, reporting route, consent)
      • Safe-practice habits (rule of two, contact, communication)
      • If a child discloses harm
      • Girls’ cricket
      • Disability / para-cricket
      • STEP framework (Space, Task, Equipment, People)
    • Ch 7 · Fast-Bowling Workload
      • Match limits: overs per spell & per day by age
      • Rest-between-spells rule
      • Weekly load & recovery
      • Warning signs of back injury
      • Mixed-action risk
    • Ch 8 · Injury, Equipment & the Field
      • Mandatory helmets (batting & keeping up)
      • Young-fielder distances (11 yd U13 / 8 yd U15)
      • RAMP warm-up
      • Common injuries & concussion protocol
      • Heat, hydration, ground safety, Emergency Action Plan
  • PART 3 · Technical Skills
    • Ch 9 · Batting
      • Grip (V-alignment)
      • Stance & set-up (still, level head)
      • Backlift & the ‘V’
      • Reading length → footwork
      • Shots in layers: defence, drives, cut/pull, sweep
      • Running between the wickets
      • Power hitting
      • Fault diagnosis
    • Ch 10 · Pace Bowling
      • Action phases (run-up, gather, delivery stride, release, follow-through)
      • Braced front leg
      • Alignment: side-on / midway / front-on vs mixed action
      • Grip, seam & swing (out / in)
      • Length, line & the corridor
      • Variations (slower ball, yorker, bouncer, cutter)
      • Fault diagnosis
    • Ch 11 · Spin Bowling
      • Finger spin (off-break) vs wrist spin (leg-break)
      • Grips & release
      • Weapons in the air: flight, dip, drift, turn
      • Tempting length
      • Variations (arm ball, googly, top-spinner, flipper)
      • Fields & tactics; rip it first
      • Fault diagnosis
    • Ch 12 · Fielding
      • Catching (high, flat, close) & the cups
      • Ground fielding (long barrier, attacking pick-up, one-handed)
      • Throwing & the crow-hop
      • Run-outs & backing up
      • Fielding positions map
      • Team fielding & energy
      • Fault diagnosis
    • Ch 13 · Wicketkeeping
      • Stance & rising with the bounce
      • Standing back vs standing up
      • Soft hands / the give
      • Footwork (feet then hands)
      • Stumpings & run-outs
      • Keeper safety (helmet standing up)
      • Fault diagnosis
